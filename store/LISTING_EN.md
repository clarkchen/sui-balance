See your Sui balances in one clear popup.

Sui Balance shows coin balances for Sui Mainnet addresses and objects. Open a Suiscan account or object page to detect its ID automatically, or enter any Sui address or object ID yourself.

• View total address balances by coin type, including Coin objects and Sui address balances.
• See internal balances for supported Vault objects in a separate section.
• Read amounts using on-chain coin decimals. When decimals are unavailable, raw base units are clearly labeled.
• Tell apart coins that share a symbol by their package ID.

Sui Balance is a read-only viewer. It does not connect to a wallet, request private keys, or estimate fiat value. It does not calculate positions in other DeFi protocols.

Queries use the public Sui GraphQL endpoint. Results may be affected by indexing delays or rate limits.

Open source: https://github.com/clarkchen/sui-balance
